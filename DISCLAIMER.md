# Disclaimer

**OverheatLens is open research and decision-support software. It implements published
overheating and thermal-comfort methods and can orchestrate EnergyPlus simulations, but it is
not a certified compliance certificate. Regulatory requirements, approved documents, weather
datasets and industry guidance change over time. Formal planning or Building Control submissions
must use the applicable current requirements and be reviewed or signed off by a suitably
qualified professional.**

Additional specific notices:

- Standards implementations whose source documents have not yet been verified against the
  originals are labelled "source not verified" and cannot be used for compliance-labelled
  results within the software.
- Results depend entirely on the quality and appropriateness of the supplied weather file and
  building model. OverheatLens reports input problems; it cannot guarantee input validity.
- Cross-software comparisons may differ because of simulation-engine and model-translation
  differences as well as post-processing differences; OverheatLens separates and reports these.
